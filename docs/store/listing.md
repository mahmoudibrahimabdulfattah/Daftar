«دفتر» دفتر خاص لأصولك، يجمع الذهب والحسابات البنكية والنقد والاستثمارات وسجلات الزكاة والملاحظات. تسجّل ما لديك يدويًا، وتراجع قيمة أصولك بالجنيه المصري في مكان واحد.

أسعار السوق المصرية

حماية سجلاتك ونسخك

تُحفظ سجلاتك مشفّرة على جهازك خلف رمز خاص بـ«دفتر»، مع فتح بالبصمة على الأجهزة الداعمة. يُقفل التطبيق فور انتقاله إلى الخلفية، ويمنع لقطات الشاشة ويغطي محتواه في شاشة التطبيقات الأخيرة.

يمكنك تصدير نسخة مشفّرة بصيغة ⁦.dftar⁩، أو اختيار النسخ السحابية المشفّرة عبر حساب Google. كلمة مرور النسخة مستقلة عن رمز التطبيق، ولا تقل عن ⁦12⁩ حرفًا. تُشفّر النسخة قبل رفعها بطلبك؛ لا توجد مزامنة تلقائية، ولا تحتاج إلى حساب لاستخدام الدفتر على جهازك.

بلا إعلانات أو اتصال بحساباتك البنكية أو حسابات الوساطة. لا ينفّذ «دفتر» تحويلات أو تداولًا، ولا يقدّم نصائح مالية. متاح بالعربية والإنجليزية، بمظهر فاتح وداكن.

## English

**App name — 23/30 characters**

Daftar: Wealth Notebook

**Short description — 72/80 characters**

Track gold, balances, investments and zakat in a private wealth notebook

**Full description — 2,860/4,000 characters**

Daftar is a private notebook for your assets: gold, bank accounts, cash, investments, zakat records and notes. Record what you own manually and see its combined value in Egyptian pounds.

Every balance in its place

• Gold: record bars and jewelry by karat and net weight. Holdings are valued at the goldsmith’s sell price to you, shown as “Sells to you”, excluding making charges and fees. This is not the price you would receive when selling your gold.
• Bank accounts: record EGP and USD balances, with their equivalent value in Egyptian pounds. No account numbers or bank login details are needed.
• Cash and wallets: keep a record of cash on hand and wallet balances.
• Investments: enter your investment account’s current value in EGP and its valuation date. You update this value yourself.
• Notes: keep details you want to remember, such as a receipt’s location or a personal loan. Amounts in notes do not contribute to total assets.

Egyptian market prices

Daftar fetches live gold and USD prices online and shows source and update details. If a refresh fails, it uses the last cached prices, with manual prices as a fallback when no source price is available. You can also pin a manual price until you choose to return to the source. Prices are valuation references, not guaranteed buying or selling rates.

Zakat and the Hijri calendar

Record zakat payments, mark each year’s completion yourself and review the next Ramadan date using the civil Hijri calendar. You can adjust the date after Egypt’s moon-sighting announcement; it may differ by a day.

The calculator provides a planning estimate of 2.5% of all recorded assets, including jewelry and investments. It does not assess nisab or hawl, deduct debts, apply exemptions or determine the zakat treatment of your investments. It is not a fatwa. Recording payments does not automatically reduce your balances.

Protect your records and backups

Your records are stored encrypted on your device behind a separate Daftar PIN, with biometric unlock on supported devices. The app locks immediately when it enters the background, blocks screenshots and covers its content in recent apps.

Export an encrypted .dftar file, or choose optional encrypted cloud backups with Google sign-in. Backups use a separate password of at least 12 characters, distinct from your app PIN. Encryption happens before an upload you request. There is no automatic sync, and no account is needed to use your notebook on your device.

The Android app uses Firebase for crash reports, usage analytics and update checks. Your balances and notes are not added to crash reports or analytics. The privacy policy explains what data is sent.

No ads, bank connections or brokerage connections. Daftar does not transfer money, execute trades or provide financial advice. Available in Arabic and English, with light and dark themes.
